# Home insurance policy wording

## What is covered

Your home policy covers sudden and accidental damage to the buildings and contents, including escape of
water from a burst pipe, fire, storm and theft after forced entry.

## Escape of water

We pay for repairing the damage caused by water escaping from fixed pipes and tanks. The excess for escape
of water claims is 350 EUR. Gradual leaks that were not repaired are not covered.

## What is not covered

Wear and tear, damage caused by pets, and losses while the home is unoccupied for more than 60 days.
