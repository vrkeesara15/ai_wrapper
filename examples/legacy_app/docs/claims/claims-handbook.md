# Claims handling handbook

## Registering a claim

Every claim gets a claim number (CLM-....) and is linked to an active policy. Lapsed policies are not eligible.

## Settlement authority

Adjusters may settle claims up to 5000 EUR. Claims above 5000 EUR need approval from a senior adjuster.
Claims above 10000 EUR are reviewed by the claims committee every Thursday.

## Closing a claim

A claim is closed once payment is confirmed and the customer has been informed in writing.
